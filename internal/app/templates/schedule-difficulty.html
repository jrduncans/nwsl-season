Remaining schedule difficulty starts with each opponent’s current points per game (PPG), adjusts for whether that opponent will play at home or away, and then adjusts for the two teams’ relative fixture load entering that match. Short recovery raises pressure between six and five elapsed days and saturates at five days; a third or later match within nine elapsed days adds accumulated load. A team’s schedule gets harder when it is more loaded than its opponent, easier when its opponent is more loaded, and no load adjustment when both are equally loaded. Higher values mean a harder remaining schedule.

For each fixture, the venue-adjusted opponent PPG is multiplied by exp(team congestion − opponent congestion). Congestion is 0.04 × recovery pressure + 0.075 for a third-or-later match within nine days. These are the same strongly shrunk effects used by the selected Forecast Lab model. The venue adjustment uses {{.Strength.CompletedMatches}} completed regular-season matches from this season and up to the two previous seasons: home teams average {{.Strength.HomePPG}} points per game and away teams average {{.Strength.AwayPPG}}, for a {{.Strength.VenueGap}}-point gap.
