Remaining schedule difficulty is the average points per game (PPG) of a team’s remaining opponents, adjusted for home and away matches. For each fixture, we start with the opponent’s current PPG. Each remaining fixture is adjusted separately: in a home match, the opponent is playing away, so we subtract half the difference between the league’s average home and away PPG from the opponent’s value. In an away match, the opponent is playing at home, so we add half that difference. Higher values mean a harder remaining schedule.

The adjustment uses {{.Strength.CompletedMatches}} completed matches: home teams average {{.Strength.HomePPG}} points per game and away teams average {{.Strength.AwayPPG}}, for a {{.Strength.VenueGap}}-point gap.
