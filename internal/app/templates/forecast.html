    Methodology and limitations

    {{.ModelName}} {{.ModelDetail}}

    Unfixed scores are independent Poisson samples. A fixed win, draw, or loss samples a plausible scoreline conditional on that outcome, so goal-based tiebreakers retain uncertainty.

    The random seed comes from the model version, cached fixture snapshot, and fixed assumptions. Shared links use the latest cached data rather than preserving a historical snapshot; assumptions for a completed fixture become stale.

    Where cached data cannot resolve an official disciplinary-points tiebreak, the forecast splits probability across the tied positions. Forecast probabilities are not mathematical clinching proofs.
