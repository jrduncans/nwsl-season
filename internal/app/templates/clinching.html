About chances and the table

Rows show overall finish chances. Column headings show own-results chances among counted forecasts; cell percentages are the chance of both the finish and those results. Opponent percentages below a finish are the chance of that finish AND opponent. These joint chances are not conditional probabilities, and rounded percentages are not normalized.{{if .LikelihoodModel}} Forecast: {{.LikelihoodModel}}{{if .Builder.FixedCount}}, given the fixed results{{end}}.{{end}}

Team results focuses the table only; use Fix results to change the scenario. Choose a finish to see its scenarios.

Cells list possible first-round opponents, likeliest first when chances are available. Every listed finish is exactly possible, however unlikely. The higher seed normally hosts; venue availability can change hosting.
